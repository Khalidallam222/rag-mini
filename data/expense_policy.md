# Employee Expense Policy

This document is a fictional sample policy for Nile Trading Co., written for learning purposes.

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains which business expenses the company pays back to employees and how to claim them.

## 2. Submitting a claim
Employees submit expense claims in the expense system within 30 days of the expense date. Every expense above EGP 200 needs an original receipt or a clear photo of it. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless the finance manager approves an exception.

## 3. Meals
When travelling for work inside Egypt, employees may claim up to EGP 600 per day for meals. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client entertainment meals need the department manager's approval in advance and the names of the clients must be written on the claim.

## 4. Transport
Employees should use the company car service or ride-hailing apps for business trips inside Cairo. Personal car use is paid at EGP 5 per kilometre. Taxi or ride-hailing receipts must show the date, start point and end point.

## 5. Hotels and travel outside Cairo
Hotel bookings are made by the HR travel desk, not by the employee. The maximum hotel rate is EGP 3,500 per night inside Egypt. International travel must be approved by the CEO at least 14 days before the trip.

## 6. Payment of claims
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary. The finance team can ask for more information; if the employee does not reply within 10 working days, the claim is closed.
