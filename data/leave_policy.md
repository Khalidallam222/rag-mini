# Leave Policy

This document is a fictional sample policy for Nile Trading Co., written for learning purposes.

## 1. Annual leave
Employees receive 21 working days of paid annual leave per year. After 10 years of service, or for employees over 50 years old, annual leave increases to 30 working days. Leave requests are submitted in SuccessFactors at least 7 days in advance and approved by the direct manager.

## 2. Carrying over leave
Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be used before the end of March, otherwise they are lost.

## 3. Sick leave
Employees must inform their manager before 10:00 AM on the first day of sickness. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## 4. Casual leave
Employees may take up to 6 days of casual leave per year for urgent personal reasons, with a maximum of 2 days at a time. Casual leave is deducted from the annual leave balance.

## 5. Working from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with their manager's approval. Working from home is not allowed during the first 3 months of employment.
